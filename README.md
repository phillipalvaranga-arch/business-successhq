# Business SuccessHQ

An original planning app for any business idea. Capture ideas, score them, validate them, write a plan, follow official checklists, and track goals, habits, and numbers you enter yourself. Video notes let you paste a YouTube summary or import a PDF, Word, or Excel file, write how you will use it in your business, and keep step-by-step instructions you can check off — all on this device. Imported files are read in the browser and are not uploaded.

Live: https://phillipalvaranga-arch.github.io/business-successhq/

This app is not affiliated with The Success Playbook YouTube channel (@successplaybookhq) or any other similarly named company or show.

The Ideas section includes a “From SuccessPlaybookHQ” collection summarized from that channel’s public videos. Ideas you add from it go into your idea bank with an empty scorecard. Ideas summarized from The Success Playbook YouTube channel; not affiliated.

Your information stays on this device (in the browser). It is not sent to a server. Do not enter Social Security numbers, full Employer Identification Numbers, bank or card numbers, or passwords.

This app does not give legal, tax, financial, or trading advice, and it does not place trades or file forms.
