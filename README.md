# Business SuccessHQ

An original planning app for any business idea. Capture ideas, score them, validate them, write a plan, follow official checklists, and track goals, habits, and numbers you enter yourself.

Live: https://phillipalvaranga-arch.github.io/business-successhq/

This app is not affiliated with The Success Playbook YouTube channel (@successplaybookhq) or any other similarly named company or show.

Your information stays on this device (in the browser). It is not sent to a server. Do not enter Social Security numbers, full Employer Identification Numbers, bank or card numbers, or passwords.

This app does not give legal, tax, financial, or trading advice, and it does not place trades or file forms.
